Open Now: Summer Internship Program

Agentic AI Engineer Intern (Summer, 2 Months)

Internship 2 Months Summer 2026 India

You will help design and ship agentic workflows that read context, choose tools, call APIs, and automate real workflows without falling apart when inputs get messy. The internship blends guided learning with hands-on product building for additional internal platforms that we use in day-to-day operations.

  • Build and test multi-step AI agents for real business tasks, not toy prompts.
  • Work with Python, FastAPI, JavaScript/TypeScript, GitHub, and practical deployment pipelines.
  • Use OpenAI and Anthropic APIs with orchestration tools such as LangChain and LangGraph.
  • Implement retrieval, tracing, evaluations, and guardrails so outputs are reliable.
  • Ship modules that expand internal platforms for automation, analytics, and velocity.
  • Learn by building: every week includes mentor checkpoints, feedback loops, and shipped output.

Ideal for people who like turning chaos into systems, ask sharp questions, and enjoy the moment when a flaky workflow becomes boringly reliable.

Apply on our hiring portal (link to be added). This website only publishes role details.

Bonus points if you have built side projects where bots call tools, maintain memory, or integrate with external apps.
